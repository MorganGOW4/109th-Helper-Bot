# Privacy Policy for Discord Bot

**Last Updated:** August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Discord bot ("109th Helper Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and Discord's Developer Terms.

---

### 1. Data Controller and Contact
The Data Controller responsible for your personal data is the Bot Developer. If you have any questions or wish to exercise your data rights, contact us at:
* **Contact Email:** morgangow4@gmail.com

---

### 2. Data We Collect and How We Use It
To provide core functionality, moderation tools, and logging, the Bot processes and stores the following information:

* **Discord Identifiers:** We collect your unique Discord User ID, Username, Server/Guild ID, Role IDs, and Channel IDs. 
  * *Purpose:* To accurately track permissions, update server roles, and execute moderation actions (such as kicking users).
* **Message Content & Metadata:** The Bot scans text message content in channels it has access to. 
  * *Purpose:* Specific aspects of your message content are parsed to execute commands or maintain designated server records.
* **Server Moderation Logs:** Actions taken by or through the Bot (such as kicking a user or updating a role) are recorded.
  * *Purpose:* To ensure accountability and maintain a chronological safety log for server administrators.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Third-Party Sharing
We treat your data with strict confidentiality. It is distributed and stored as follows:

* **Google Sheets (External Database):** Selected data parsed from processed message content, alongside relevant User and Server IDs, is written directly to a secure Google Sheets spreadsheet for long-term administrative logging.
* **Discord Channels (Internal Logs):** Other aspects of parsed message activity and moderation history (such as kick logs and role updates) are posted publicly or privately to designated logging channels within the specific Discord server.
* **Third-Party Infrastructure:** Data is handled by our hosting environment and transmitted via Discord’s API and Google Workspace APIs. We never sell, rent, or trade your data to outside commercial entities.

---

### 4. Legal Basis for Processing (UK GDPR)
Under the UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases to process your information:
* **Legitimate Interests:** To provide essential automation, tracking features, and server moderation tools requested by the server administrators to keep the Discord community secure and functional.

---

### 5. Data Retention
We only store your data for as long as it is necessary to fulfil its intended administrative or moderation tracking purposes. 
* Logs within Discord channels remain until deleted by a server administrator.
* Data stored in Google Sheets will be pruned or anonymized if the Bot is removed from the server or upon a verified user request.

---

### 6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR
As a UK resident, you hold specific legal rights regarding your personal data:
* **Right of Access:** You can request a copy of the data we hold about you.
* **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** You can request that we delete your logged data from our Google Sheets database.
* **Right to Restrict Processing:** You can request that we temporarily halt processing your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the developer via the email listed in Section 1 or leave the server to stop active processing.

---

### 7. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Bot after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
